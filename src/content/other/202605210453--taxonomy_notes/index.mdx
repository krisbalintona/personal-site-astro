Immediate writing shared with little refinement. This includes reflections, observations, ongoing thinking, and random tidbits.

These posts present practically unedited and often half-baked thoughts. As such, they can be very short, very long, or anything in between. The common denominator is that they preserve thinking nearer to its original form. They tend to be mere personal writings that I share just in case someone else finds it interesting or useful.
