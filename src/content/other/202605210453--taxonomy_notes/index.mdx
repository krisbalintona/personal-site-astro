Immediate writing shared with little refinement. This includes reflections, observations, ongoing thinking, and random tidbits.

These posts are records of my thinking. They present practically unedited writing and often half-baked thoughts as they unfold. As such, they can be very short, very long, or anything in between. The common denominator is that they preserve thinking nearer to its original moment. They tend to be mere personal writings that I share just in case someone else finds it interesting or useful.
