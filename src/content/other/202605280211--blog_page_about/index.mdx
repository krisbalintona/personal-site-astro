This site is handmade with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and hosted for free on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/). All of its source code may be found [on Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/personal-site-astro.git). Thanks to Astro, it is statically built and ships with nearly no JavaScript. Your privacy is respected: this site uses no cookies and collects no personal data. [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) provides basic, anonymized analytics.

Aside from rearranging the pages, redoing styling, and writing my own components, the move from Hugo to Astro came with various additions and improvements to the site. This includes:

-   Tag-specific RSS feeds
-   Per-page emoji reactions
-   Publishing "live" documents

Content is written in [Org-mode](https://orgmode.org/), exported to MDX files using a custom [`ox-mdx.el` org-mode export backend](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/personal-site-astro/src/branch/main/scripts/org-exporter/ox-mdx.el) developed specifically for this site.
