This site is an attempt to think publicly and stay engaged with the things that matter to me&mdash;might as well share them with others along the way. Publicly sharing artifacts of thought was central to Niklas Luhmann's zettelkasten practice, a practice I've taken great inspiration from over the years.

I use [Emacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emacs) for most things. On it I lightly code, write notes, and manage work. Some of the projects I've authored or maintain:

-   [vc-jj.el: A GNU Emacs vc.el integration with Jujutsu VCS](https://codeberg.org/emacs-jj-vc/vc-jj.el)
-   [org-hide-drawers: Hide drawers in org-mode with overlays](https://github.com/krisbalintona/org-hide-drawers)
-   [My Emacs config](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/emacs-config)
-   [My Guix config](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/guix-config)

This site is handmade with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and hosted for free on [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/). All of its source code may be found [on Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/personal-site-astro.git). Thanks to Astro, it is statically built and ships with nearly no JavaScript. Content is written in [Org-mode](https://orgmode.org/), exported to MDX files using my custom [`ox-mdx.el` org-mode export backend](https://codeberg.org/krisbalintona/personal-site-astro/src/branch/main/scripts/org-exporter/ox-mdx.el).
