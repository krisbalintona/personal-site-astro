Emacs is hard to put in a box. On one level, it is the most extensible text editor, used for programming, writing code, wrangling todos, reading and writing email, reading RSS feeds, and more. On another level, it's an Emacs-lisp *interpreter*. And more abstractly, it's an digital living space integrated under a single UX.
